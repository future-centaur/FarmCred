## DOMAIN GLOSSARY — Kenyan smallholder agricultural credit, 2026-11-10

Canonical terms for this slice of the field. App names come later. Terms where everyday speech diverges from the domain are marked.

| term | definition | flag |
|---|---|---|
| Smallholder | A farmer seeking credit against a small plot’s season, not a commercial estate. | |
| Plot | The piece of land being farmed. Located by a GPS pin captured on the phone. | “Farm” in speech can mean the household, the business, or this pin. Here it is the pin and the area on it. |
| Ward | Administrative location under a sub-county. The unit for climate normals and county yield lookups. | Not the plot, and not a postal address. |
| National identity | Who the person is in law, checked by comparing the name they give, the document, and a registry. | |
| Tenure | The holding: owned, leased, family land, allotment letter, or title. | Everyday “I own the shamba” covers family land and allotment letters that are not title. |
| Production capacity | How much the plot can produce. A ceiling on repayment capacity. | Not character and not willingness to repay. |
| Yield | Physical volume of a season. | Not revenue. A full harvest can still be unpaid. |
| Realized revenue | KES actually received from a buyer or cooperative for that output. | Not yield. |
| Offtake / market linkage | The sales relationship, graded from spot market only to a signed offtake. | “Has a buyer” is only the cheap first question. Delivered volume is the later one. |
| Delivery record | What was delivered to a cooperative or processor, by season, with value. | No record is “none,” not “bad.” |
| Mobile-money cash flow | Level and regularity of inflows on a statement the farmer consented to. | Not the same as farm revenue. Airtime, transfers, and a cooperative paybill sit on one statement. |
| Debt-service ratio | Proposed instalment divided by median monthly inflow. | A proxy. It is only as good as the inflow figure and the optional side-income amount. |
| Loan exposure | Debt outstanding now. | Not a repayment history. |
| Repayment performance | How earlier credit was paid. | Not the current balance. |
| Credit-reference listing | Customer information at a bureau, including adverse information as the regulations define it. | A bureau “credit score” is their number. It is not this product’s farm evidence score. |
| Negative information | The regulatory list: non-performing facilities, fraud, bankruptcy, and the other items the Credit Reference Bureau Regulations name. | Not a synonym for “risky farmer.” |
| PAYGo | Pay-as-you-go finance on a household or farm device, paid in small regular amounts. | Not a mobile-lender cash loan. The device can also show irrigation. |
| Chama | An informal savings and lending group, including table banking. | Joining is not the same as paying. |
| SACCO | A regulated or formal savings and credit cooperative. | Not a chama, and not a mobile wallet. |
| SACCO savings balance | Stock of savings on the cooperative’s books. | Not wallet savings frequency. |
| Social collateral | A group’s track record, a guarantee, or a named referee standing behind the farmer. | Not land title. |
| Reference contact | A person the farmer types in, who can be called. | Not a row from the phone book. |
| Climate exposure | The ward’s historical weather risk. | Decides size, term, and insurance. Does not decide accept or decline. |
| NDVI anomaly | This plot this season versus its own vegetation baseline. | Not the ward climate score, and a missing series is not zero. |
| Rainfall deficit | This season’s rain so far versus the ward’s long-run average. | A monitoring signal. Not a decline reason. |
| Irrigation access | The plot has irrigation. Captured once. | |
| Eligibility gate | Pass or fail, outside the points. | Not a heavy negative weight. |
| Input loan | Credit for the crop cycle: seed, fertilizer, equipment, labour. | Repays differently from a general cash loan. |
| Expected harvest date | When the crop calendar says cash can exist. | Not the month the farmer prefers to repay. |
| Preferred repayment month | The month the farmer asks for. | Checked against harvest. Not replaced by it. |
| Field attestation | A person on the ground confirms a claim. | The route for tenure, because title search is out. |
| Consent | A specific yes for a named use, which the controller can prove. | A blanket tick is not consent for every category. |
| Sensitive personal data | As the Data Protection Act defines it, including property details, marital status, named family members, and sex. | “Gender” in the research is the fairness variable. The Act’s word for that category is sex. They are not silently the same field. |
| Serviceable area | Where the lender will actually operate. | Not “wards with hard weather.” |
| KIAMIS | The national farmer registry used as a background check on the farmer and parcel. | Not the land-title registry. |
| Digital credit provider | A person the Central Bank licenses under the 2022 digital-credit regulations. | Not automatically whoever writes a score. Banks, microfinance institutions, and SACCOs have their own regimes. |
